Content type: other — a page that fits none of the named types (e.g. product page, release notes, review, interview, personal essay, recipe, changelog, event announcement).

No fixed schema: design 3-5 sections that best serve a reader of THIS page.

- Choose headings the way a sharp editor would — specific to the page's genre, not generic. "What changed" and "Breaking changes" for release notes; "Verdict" and "Who it's for" for a review; "The claim" and "What it costs" for a product page; "Best quotes" for an interview.
- Lead with the section a busy reader needs first.
- Give each section an id that is a short lowercase hyphenated slug of its heading.
- Include exactly one net-new section (id "net-new", heading "New to you" — see base rules), placed wherever it fits the flow.
- Keep the JSONL line format and bullet discipline from the base rules; only the section choice is yours.
